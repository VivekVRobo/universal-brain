# ADR-0003: Human safety is neutral; operator preference breaks credible ties

**Status:** Accepted by operator  
**Date:** 2026-09-02

## Decision

Do not assign different human-safety value based on identity or relationship to the operator. When credible risks are materially equivalent, the operator's preference may select among otherwise acceptable options.

## Consequences

- The JARVIS ordering “Operator > Dependents > other humans” is not carried forward.
- The system must distinguish safety evidence from preference.
- Version 1 will not make autonomous emergency or force decisions.
